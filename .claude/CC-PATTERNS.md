# CC-PATTERNS.md - Retention & Engagement Patterns

Proven patterns to maximize retention, engagement and virality.

## Pattern Philosophy

```yaml
core_principle: "Attention is earned every second"
retention_goal: ">70% watch time"
engagement_goal: ">5% engagement rate"
viral_threshold: "Share rate > comment rate"
```

## Hook Patterns (0-3 seconds)

### Pattern: Curiosity Gap
```yaml
pattern: curiosity-gap
mechanism: "Creates an information gap that the audience wants to fill"
power: 9/10
risk: "Clickbait if no payoff"

templates:
  - "What nobody tells you about [topic]"
  - "The secret that [authority] doesn't want you to know"
  - "I discovered why [common problem]"
  - "Here's what really happens when [action]"
  - "The real reason why [phenomenon]"

execution:
  visual: "Intrigued expression / reaction shot"
  tone: "Confidential, conspiratorial"
  energy: "Mid-high"
  follow_up: "MUST deliver the answer (no bait)"
```

### Pattern: Controversial Statement
```yaml
pattern: controversial
mechanism: "Triggers immediate emotional reaction (agreement/disagreement)"
power: 9/10
risk: "Polarizing, can backfire"

templates:
  - "[Unpopular opinion] and I'm not sorry"
  - "Stop [popular advice]"
  - "[Common belief] is a lie"
  - "I'm going to get hated for this but [truth bomb]"
  - "Why I refuse to [norm]"

execution:
  visual: "Direct camera, confident"
  tone: "Assured, not aggressive"
  energy: "High"
  follow_up: "Strong arguments to support"
```

### Pattern: Story Tease
```yaml
pattern: story-tease
mechanism: "Opens a narrative loop that the audience wants to see closed"
power: 9/10
risk: "Must deliver satisfying ending"

templates:
  - "I lost everything when [setup]..."
  - "This morning, I received a message that [tease]"
  - "I should never have [action], here's what happened"
  - "The worst mistake of my life"
  - "How I went from [bad] to [good]"

execution:
  visual: "Emotional, vulnerable or shocked"
  tone: "Personal, authentic"
  energy: "Variable based on emotion"
  follow_up: "Complete story with narrative arc"
```

### Pattern: Direct Value Promise
```yaml
pattern: value-promise
mechanism: "Clear promise of benefit in exchange for attention"
power: 7/10
risk: "Generic if poorly executed"

templates:
  - "3 ways to [benefit] in [time]"
  - "How I [achievement] without [common method]"
  - "The framework I use for [result]"
  - "[Number] mistakes that [negative consequence]"
  - "Do this if you want [desired outcome]"

execution:
  visual: "Visual list or demonstration"
  tone: "Expert, helpful"
  energy: "Mid"
  follow_up: "Deliver exactly what was promised"
```

### Pattern: Pattern Interrupt
```yaml
pattern: interrupt-shock
mechanism: "Breaks expectations and forces attention"
power: 8/10
risk: "Can seem gimmicky"

templates:
  - "STOP. Before scrolling..."
  - "[Unexpected action] + now that I have your attention"
  - "[Surprising sound/visual] + ok, let's talk about..."
  - "You're going to hate me for this"
  - "[Weird gesture/action]"

execution:
  visual: "Quick movement, sudden change"
  tone: "Urgent, direct"
  energy: "High burst"
  follow_up: "Quick transition to value content"
```

### Pattern: Identity Call-Out
```yaml
pattern: identity-callout
mechanism: "Directly calls out a specific group"
power: 8/10
risk: "Excludes those who don't identify"

templates:
  - "If you're [identity], you need to know this"
  - "[Group] will understand"
  - "POV: you're [specific situation]"
  - "This is for [group] who [struggle]"
  - "Tag someone who [behavior]"

execution:
  visual: "Direct, inclusive"
  tone: "Like a friend who understands"
  energy: "Mid, relatable"
  follow_up: "Ultra-relevant content for this group"
```

## Retention Patterns (Mid-Content)

### Pattern: Open Loop
```yaml
pattern: open-loop
mechanism: "Tease future information to maintain attention"
power: 9/10
placement: "Every 15-30 seconds"

techniques:
  verbal:
    - "...but before I tell you that, you need to understand..."
    - "...and the craziest part is what happened next..."
    - "...I'm going to reveal [thing] in a second, but first..."
    - "...wait until you see what happens..."

  visual:
    - "Text overlay teasing what's coming"
    - "Quick flash of end result"
    - "Progress bar / countdown"

  structural:
    - "Promise in hook → Deliver at 70%"
    - "Multiple mini-reveals throughout"
    - "Delayed payoff technique"

execution_rules:
  - "Open max 2-3 loops simultaneously"
  - "Close loops before opening new ones (mostly)"
  - "Final loop = main hook payoff"
  - "Satisfying closure is NON-NEGOTIABLE"
```

### Pattern: Pattern Interrupt (Mid)
```yaml
pattern: mid-interrupt
mechanism: "Reset attention that naturally decreases"
power: 8/10
frequency: "Every 7-15 seconds"

techniques:
  visual:
    - "Jump cut"
    - "Angle change"
    - "B-roll insertion"
    - "Zoom in/out"
    - "Text pop"
    - "Color shift"

  audio:
    - "Music change"
    - "Sound effect"
    - "Voice modulation"
    - "Silence beat"

  content:
    - "Tangent then return"
    - "Quick joke"
    - "Self-interruption"
    - "Audience callout"

timing_guide:
  short_form: "Every 5-10s"
  mid_form: "Every 10-20s"
  long_form: "Every 20-45s"
```

### Pattern: Value Stacking
```yaml
pattern: value-stack
mechanism: "Each segment adds value, creating obligation to continue"
power: 8/10

structure:
  segment_1: "Quick win (easy value)"
  segment_2: "Deeper insight (builds on #1)"
  segment_3: "Advanced tip (reward for staying)"
  bonus: "Secret/exclusive (final payoff)"

techniques:
  - "Visible numbering (1/3, 2/3, 3/3)"
  - "Value escalation ('and that's nothing compared to...')"
  - "Callback to previous points"
  - "Promise of best at end"

verbal_bridges:
  - "Ok that was the basics, now the real game..."
  - "And this is where it gets interesting..."
  - "But the tip that changes everything..."
  - "What pros never tell you..."
```

### Pattern: Story Peaks
```yaml
pattern: story-peaks
mechanism: "Emotional peaks that renew investment"
power: 9/10

emotional_peaks:
  tension: "Build anxiety/anticipation"
  revelation: "Surprise/twist moment"
  victory: "Success/achievement"
  vulnerability: "Raw honesty/failure"
  humor: "Comedic relief"

placement:
  - "Major peak at 70-80% of content"
  - "Mini peaks every 20-30%"
  - "Closing peak before CTA"

construction:
  buildup: "3-5 seconds of rising tension"
  peak: "1-2 second climax"
  release: "2-3 seconds aftermath"
  bridge: "Transition to next segment"
```

## Engagement Patterns

### Pattern: Comment Bait
```yaml
pattern: comment-bait
mechanism: "Creates an irresistible reason to comment"
power: 8/10

techniques:
  opinion_ask:
    - "Team [A] or Team [B]?"
    - "Do you agree or not?"
    - "Tell me in comments if..."

  fill_blank:
    - "Complete: [start of sentence]..."
    - "The first word that comes to mind is..."

  challenge:
    - "Prove me wrong"
    - "Change my mind"
    - "Can someone explain..."

  community:
    - "Tag someone who..."
    - "If you see this, comment [word]"
    - "[Group] will understand"

placement:
  - "Early comment bait (0-30s) = boost algorithm"
  - "Mid content = engagement spike"
  - "End = CTA complement"
```

### Pattern: Save Trigger
```yaml
pattern: save-trigger
mechanism: "Gives a reason to save for later"
power: 9/10 (especially Instagram)

techniques:
  utility:
    - "Save for when you need it"
    - "This is the kind of thing you'll want to find again"

  density:
    - "Too much info to remember"
    - "List/checklist format"
    - "Step-by-step guide"

  reference:
    - "Reusable template"
    - "Applicable framework"
    - "Reminder for later"

content_types_high_save:
  - "Tutorials / How-to"
  - "Lists / Resources"
  - "Templates / Frameworks"
  - "Checklists"
  - "Quotes / Inspiration"
```

### Pattern: Share Trigger
```yaml
pattern: share-trigger
mechanism: "Creates a reason to share with others"
power: 10/10 (viral key)

triggers:
  identity_signal:
    - "Content that says something about the sharer"
    - "POV that resonates with a group"
    - "Achievement/transformation worth showing"

  utility_share:
    - "This would help my friend [X]"
    - "My [relation] needs to see this"
    - "Tag someone who [situation]"

  emotional_share:
    - "This made me feel [emotion]"
    - "I've never felt so seen"
    - "This is too good not to share"

  controversy_share:
    - "Look at this hot take"
    - "Do you agree with this?"
    - "This is going to upset people"

content_types_high_share:
  - "Relatable struggles"
  - "Controversial opinions"
  - "Useful resources"
  - "Emotional stories"
  - "Humor/memes"
```

## Viral Patterns

### Pattern: Trend Hijack
```yaml
pattern: trend-hijack
mechanism: "Uses existing momentum with unique angle"
power: Variable (timing-dependent)

execution:
  find: "Trending sound/format/topic"
  adapt: "Apply to your niche"
  differentiate: "Add unique twist"
  time: "Execute within 24-72h of emergence"

success_factors:
  - "Trend fit with brand (must feel natural)"
  - "Unique angle (not copy)"
  - "Quality execution (no rush jobs)"
  - "Timing (early = better)"

risk_mitigation:
  - "Only jump on brand-fit trends"
  - "Add genuine value, not just participation"
  - "Avoid forced/cringe adaptations"
```

### Pattern: Reaction Content
```yaml
pattern: reaction
mechanism: "Piggyback on existing popular content"
power: 8/10

types:
  duet_stitch:
    - "React to viral content"
    - "Add expertise/perspective"
    - "Challenge or support claim"

  commentary:
    - "Analyze trending topic"
    - "Expert take on news"
    - "Counter-opinion"

success_factors:
  - "Original source is viral/trending"
  - "Your take adds genuine value"
  - "Posted within trend window"
```

### Pattern: Series Viral
```yaml
pattern: series-viral
mechanism: "Each episode drives to the next"
power: 9/10 (compounding)

structure:
  naming: "Part 1/X, Episode X, Day X"
  cliffhanger: "End each with tease for next"
  progression: "Clear value escalation"
  accessibility: "Each works standalone too"

viral_mechanics:
  - "Binge-watch behavior"
  - "Anticipation building"
  - "Comment asking for next"
  - "Share to friends to catch up"
```

## CTA Patterns

### Pattern: Soft CTA
```yaml
pattern: soft-cta
mechanism: "CTA naturally integrated into content"
power: 7/10 (high conversion, low reach impact)

techniques:
  - "If you want more [topic], you know what to do"
  - "I talk more about it on [platform]"
  - "Comment if you want the next part"
  - "Profile for [deeper content type]"

placement: "Mid-content or natural break"
frequency: "1-2 per content max"
```

### Pattern: Hard CTA
```yaml
pattern: hard-cta
mechanism: "Direct action request"
power: 6/10 (lower engagement but clear)

techniques:
  - "Subscribe if [condition]"
  - "Like and share if this helped you"
  - "Link in bio for [offer]"
  - "Comment [word] to receive [thing]"

placement: "End of content only"
frequency: "1 per content max"
```

### Pattern: Embedded CTA
```yaml
pattern: embedded-cta
mechanism: "CTA that is part of the value"
power: 9/10 (best of both worlds)

techniques:
  - "To apply this, step 1: follow for updates"
  - "Save this video for reference"
  - "Send this to someone who needs it"
  - "Part 2 available on my profile"

principle: "CTA = Natural next step, not interruption"
```

## Pattern Combinations

### High Retention Stack
```yaml
combination: retention-stack
sequence:
  0-3s: curiosity-gap | story-tease
  3-10s: value-promise | pattern-interrupt
  10-30s: open-loop + value-stack
  30-60s: story-peaks + mid-interrupt
  60s+: payoff + embedded-cta

expected_retention: ">70%"
```

### Viral Stack
```yaml
combination: viral-stack
elements:
  hook: controversial | identity-callout
  mid: share-trigger + comment-bait
  end: cliffhanger + soft-cta

expected_share_rate: ">3%"
```

### Educational Stack
```yaml
combination: edu-stack
sequence:
  hook: value-promise + curiosity-gap
  body: value-stack + save-trigger
  end: bonus-value + hard-cta

expected_save_rate: ">5%"
```

## Anti-Patterns (Avoid)

```yaml
anti_patterns:
  - name: "Slow Start"
    problem: "Content before hook"
    fix: "Hook in first 1s, ALWAYS"

  - name: "Broken Promise"
    problem: "Hook ≠ Content"
    fix: "Deliver exactly what hook promises"

  - name: "Energy Death"
    problem: "Monotone throughout"
    fix: "Pattern interrupts + energy modulation"

  - name: "Loop Abandonment"
    problem: "Open loops never closed"
    fix: "Track and close all loops"

  - name: "CTA Spam"
    problem: "Too many asks"
    fix: "1 clear CTA, embedded naturally"

  - name: "Value Delay"
    problem: "Making people wait too long"
    fix: "Value in first 10s, escalate from there"
```
